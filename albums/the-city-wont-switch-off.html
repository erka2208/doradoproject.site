Ten chapters between midnight and morning

The City Won't Switch Off follows a city through its wakeful hours: lit windows, late journeys, crowded rooms and the quieter moments between them.

The creative direction draws on the long-form development of 1990s progressive house, with deep bass, patient builds, spacious piano, dub textures and occasional spoken words. Each track is conceived as its own evolving journey.
