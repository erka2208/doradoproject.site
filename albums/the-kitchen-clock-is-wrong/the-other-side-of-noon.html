The scene behind the track

The narrator’s interrupted routine becomes a decision: a message is erased, a train leaves, and a call is made. The planned arrangement gives this turning point the album’s physical release.

The Other Side of Noon is track 6 of 7 on The Kitchen Clock Is Wrong. It follows The Weight of Quiet and leads into Everything We Leave Alight. Explore the running order for the full album story.
