The scene behind the track

The creative brief moves into adult horror: pursuit through a dark forest, abrupt shocks and the idea of a hunter remaining just out of view. It is one of the collection’s more intense concepts.

Hunted in the Black Woods is track 10 of 17 on Halloween: Night of Terror. It follows The Werewolf on the Hill (Part 2) and leads into Night of the Monsters. Explore the running order for the full Halloween journey.

The artwork interprets this track’s setting: a frantic viewpoint down a black forest path, broken branches, a looming unseen pursuer suggested by a shadow. It is an individual track illustration; the album has its own separate cover.
