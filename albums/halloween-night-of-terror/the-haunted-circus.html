The scene behind the track

The creative brief calls for a recognisable circus identity: carnival atmosphere, fairground machinery and an abandoned ring. The scene belongs to the haunted circus tradition rather than a woodland setting.

The Haunted Circus is track 1 of 17 on Halloween: Night of Terror. It opens the album and leads into The Haunted Circus (Part 2). Explore the running order for the full Halloween journey.

The artwork interprets this track’s setting: an abandoned striped circus tent at midnight, an empty ring, a weathered carousel and eerie carnival lights. It is an individual track illustration; the album has its own separate cover.
