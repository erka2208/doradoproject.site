The scene behind the track

This scene was conceived as a gathering of different creatures and horror ideas. It brings together the collection’s monster themes rather than concentrating on a single creature.

Night of the Monsters (Part 3) is track 13 of 17 on Halloween: Night of Terror. It follows Night of the Monsters (Part 2) and leads into The Screaming Asylum. Explore the running order for the full Halloween journey.

The artwork interprets this track’s setting: multiple original monstrous silhouettes gathering in a moonlit ruined courtyard, claws and eerie eyes in fog. It is an individual track illustration; the album has its own separate cover.
