The scene behind the track

The creative idea uses the familiar Halloween pumpkin as a guardian of the path. Warm lantern imagery gives this chapter a different identity from the colder cave and graveyard scenes.

The Pumpkin Guardians is track 17 of 19 on Halloween: The Dark Forest. It follows The Moonlit Graveyard (Part 2) and leads into The Pumpkin Guardians (Part 2). Explore the running order for the full Halloween journey.

The artwork interprets this track’s setting: glowing carved pumpkin guardians arranged beside an autumn forest trail, orange light and deep violet darkness. It is an individual track illustration; the album has its own separate cover.
