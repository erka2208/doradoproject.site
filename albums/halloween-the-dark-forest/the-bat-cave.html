The scene behind the track

Fluttering bat wings, a swarm passing nearby, small squeaks and the space of a damp cavern were central to the creative brief. Water and stone complete the underground setting.

The Bat Cave is track 8 of 19 on Halloween: The Dark Forest. It follows Footsteps Behind You (Part 2) and leads into The Bat Cave (Part 2). Explore the running order for the full Halloween journey.

The artwork interprets this track’s setting: a vast dark limestone cave with a swarm of fluttering bats, water droplets and cold moonlight at the entrance. It is an individual track illustration; the album has its own separate cover.
