The scene behind the track

The scene was conceived around footfalls on damp ground, leaves and cracking twigs. Changes of distance and moments of hesitation give the idea of being followed its identity.

Footsteps Behind You is track 6 of 19 on Halloween: The Dark Forest. It follows The Creaking Trees (Part 2) and leads into Footsteps Behind You (Part 2). Explore the running order for the full Halloween journey.

The artwork interprets this track’s setting: low viewpoint of boot impressions in wet earth and leaves, broken twigs on the path, a distant shadow between trees. It is an individual track illustration; the album has its own separate cover.
