The scene behind the track

The creative brief centres on an iron gate, wet leaves, wind and the threshold of an unknown woodland. Its purpose in the collection is to establish the forest before the journey moves deeper inside.

The Gate to the Dark Forest is track 1 of 19 on Halloween: The Dark Forest. It opens the album and leads into The Gate to the Dark Forest (Part 2). Explore the running order for the full Halloween journey.

The artwork interprets this track’s setting: a half-open iron gate on a wet path, twisted trees and blue-green fog. It is an individual track illustration; the album has its own separate cover.
