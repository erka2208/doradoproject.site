The scene behind the track

Water is central to the scene: ripples, a dark pond and a ghost at its edge. The creative direction gives the supernatural encounter a quieter setting than the monster and pursuit chapters.

The Ghost by the Pond is track 10 of 19 on Halloween: The Dark Forest. It follows The Bat Cave (Part 2) and leads into The Swamp Monster. Explore the running order for the full Halloween journey.

The artwork interprets this track’s setting: a translucent ghost beside a dark woodland pond, luminous ripples and an eerie reflection on the water. It is an individual track illustration; the album has its own separate cover.
