Under the forging press

The Press Won starts with the swing of a funk break and heads towards the clipped edges of hardcore and breakcore. Steel hits, gauges and damaged bearings gave the tracks their imagery.

Bad Teeth Concerto was conceived around a prepared grand piano against an industrial kick. The Organ Failed Inspection puts a crooked pipe-organ fugue into a gabber arrangement. Between those two, the record drops through a half-time pressure test, disco-punk and techstep.

The sleeve catches a forging press at the instant a cold die meets the hot billet. The album was created with AI tools under the creative direction of Rutger Kussendrager.
