One album, twenty different sides

Frequency Flashback starts from the pleasure of a favourite compilation: the next song can bring a different voice, a new rhythm or an unexpected change of mood. Its twenty original songs take that sense of variety into a new Dorado Project album.

Ten Dutch-language songs and ten English-language songs share the running order. From a rainy-night dance to a missed train, a road trip and a kitchen conversation, each song opens a different door.

The collection draws on the energy of 1990s and 2000s pop culture. There is room for the dance floor, guitar bands, soulful choruses and quieter moments before the communal finale.
