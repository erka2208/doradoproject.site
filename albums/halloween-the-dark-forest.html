Halloween: The Dark Forest

Electronic / Electronica / Downtempo · Halloween horror soundscapes

A 19-track Halloween journey through haunted woods, bat caves, mist, witches, water and moonlit graves. Dorado Project brings these scenes together in an electronic collection conceived around dark ambient atmosphere and sound design.

A journey into the haunted forest

Follow the album in order, from the gate into the woods, through caves and water, towards witches, a graveyard and the first hint of dawn. The collection was conceived for listeners looking for Halloween atmosphere, haunted forest ambience and dark electronic soundscapes.

Parts 2, 3 and 4 identify separate recordings of the same scene. Each recording has its own page and artwork; the original keeps its title without “Part 1”. The track order below matches the album submitted to the streaming services.
