From the last bus to first light

Current Leak follows a city after closing time. Electricity meters, an underpass, a basement and an empty railway platform give each track a different scene.

The creative direction moves between heavy breakbeats, half-spoken verses, slow dub and an instrumental jungle track. A piano-led finale brings the sequence into daylight.

The sleeve freezes a bass pulse in yellow paint. The album was created with AI tools under the direction of Rutger Kussendrager.
