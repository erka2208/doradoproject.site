Twenty stations, one shared frequency

FOREVER ON FM starts with the variety of a memorable radio afternoon: a bright synthesizer hook can lead into guitar rock, a soulful rhythm, a quiet piano or an unexpected language. Twenty new songs share that feeling while telling their own stories.

The album draws on broad musical principles heard across international 1980s hits in the Dutch Top 40. Its songs are newly written compositions rather than covers, with English, French, German and Portuguese voices across the running order.

Old photographs, a morning ferry, a found key, a family doorstep and a night bus create different windows onto ordinary lives. Friendship, humour and the courage to make a small change connect the songs.

Created with AI tools under the direction of Rutger Kussendrager. The complete 20-track album has been received by DistroKid and is processing for delivery to streaming platforms. Spotify availability has not yet been confirmed.
