ABOUT

One project.
Every direction.

Dorado Project is a genre-fluid music project. Electronic one day, rock the next — driven by an idea, a moment or a story rather than a rulebook.

Made with human direction, modern tools and absolutely no promise that the next track will sound anything like the last one.
