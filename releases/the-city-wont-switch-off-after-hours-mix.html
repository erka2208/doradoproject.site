The city after the last train

The After Hours Mix started as an extension of the album title track. The brief kept the tempo, bass pulse and central motif, made room for filtered percussion and dub delay, and let the groove carry the track into a DJ-friendly outro. Created with AI under the creative direction of Rutger Kussendrager.
