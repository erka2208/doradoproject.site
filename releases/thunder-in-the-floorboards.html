Thunder in the Floorboards

Soft rock / Classical crossover

Thunder in the Floorboards is an English-language Dorado Project single about two sisters reconnecting during a flood, conceived for cello, tenor sax and live drums.

Spotify link to follow when the track is available.

Two sisters and a photograph

The river climbs through the floorboards of their childhood home. The sisters haven’t spoken in six years, then one of them pulls a photograph from the water: two children in paper crowns. That picture became the cover. The musical direction pairs an intimate female lead and a piano-and-cello opening with the lift of 1980s soft rock. Rolling toms carry the choruses, and the tenor sax takes up the cello theme. Created with AI under the creative direction of Rutger Kussendrager.
